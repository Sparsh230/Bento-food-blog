Ingredients & Preparation

Sushi Rice: Prepared vinegared short-grain rice (the foundation of all sushi).

Vegetable Fillings:

Fresh cucumber (cut into strips)
Avocado (sliced or cut for sushi)
Other vegetarian toppings or fillings of your choice.
Nori (Seaweed Sheets): Used to wrap the rolls.
Bamboo Rolling Mat (Makisu): Essential for shaping the sushi rolls properly.

2. Rolling the Sushi

Place a sheet of Nori on your bamboo rolling mat.
Evenly spread a layer of seasoned sushi rice across the Nori, leaving a small border at the top edge.
Add your fillings (such as cucumber and avocado strips) horizontally across the center of the rice.
Using the bamboo mat, gently lift and roll the sushi away from you, applying even pressure to create a firm, compact cylinder.

3. Cutting & Plating

Cutting: Use a very sharp, slightly moistened knife to slice the rolled cylinder into neat, bite-sized pieces. Clean the blade with a wet cloth between cuts to prevent sticking.
Plating: Arrange the freshly cut vegetarian sushi rolls nicely on a plate and serve.
